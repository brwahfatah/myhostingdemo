# Database Setup

Most websites need a database to store data. Follow these steps to create and configure your database:

---

## Step 1: Access the Database Section

- Go to your **Dashboard → Databases**.

---

## Step 2: Create a New Database

- Click **Create Database**.
- Select your preferred database type: **MySQL** or **PostgreSQL**.
- Enter a database name, create a user, and set a strong password.

---

## Step 3: Configure Your Application

- Add your database credentials to your application’s configuration file.
  - For example, update the `.env` file in Laravel or the `config.php` in WordPress.

---

## Step 4: Manage Your Database

- Use **phpMyAdmin** or **Adminer** from your control panel for easy, visual management of your databases.

---

> 💡 **Tip:** Always use strong passwords and limit user privileges to enhance security.
