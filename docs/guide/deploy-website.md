# Deploy a Website

Launching your website on our hosting platform is simple and efficient. Follow these steps to get online quickly:

---

## Step 1: Log In to Your Dashboard

Access your hosting account at [your-dashboard-link] using your credentials.

---

## Step 2: Create a Hosting Space

Select the hosting type that fits your needs:

- **Shared Hosting:** Ideal for personal sites and small businesses.
- **VPS Hosting:** Perfect for larger projects requiring more control and resources.

---

## Step 3: Upload Your Website Files

Choose your preferred method:

- **FTP/SFTP:** Use an FTP client to upload files directly.
- **Git Integration:** Deploy your site using Git for streamlined updates.

---

## Step 4: Connect Your Domain

Update your domain’s DNS records to point to our servers.  
Refer to the [Domain Setup Guide](../services/domain-registration.md) for detailed instructions.

---

✅ **Your website is now live!**  
If you need help, visit our [Support Center](https://example.com/support) for expert assistance.
