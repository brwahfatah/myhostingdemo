# Account Setup

Setting up your hosting account is quick and easy. Just follow these steps:

1. **Sign Up:**  
   Visit our [Sign Up](https://example.com/signup) page and click the "Sign Up" button.

2. **Enter Your Details:**  
   Fill in the registration form with your:
   - **Full Name**
   - **Email Address** (make sure it’s valid)
   - **Password** (choose a strong, unique password)

3. **Email Verification:**  
   After submitting your details, check your inbox for a verification email. Click the link provided to activate your account.

> **Tip:** If you don’t receive the verification email within a few minutes, check your spam folder or request a new one from the sign-up page.
