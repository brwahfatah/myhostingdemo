Getting Started

Welcome! This section will guide you through the basics of using our hosting services, from creating your account to launching your first website.

Account Setup

To begin, you’ll need to create an account:

  1. Visit the Sign Up page.
  2. Fill in your details:
    • Name: Enter your full name.
    • Email: Provide a valid email address.
    • Password: Choose a strong, unique password.

Once registered, check your email for a verification link and follow the instructions to activate your account.

Accessing Your Dashboard

After activating your account, log in at the Dashboard using your email and password.
From here, you can manage your websites, view billing information, and contact support.

Launching Your First Website

  1. Click Add Website in your dashboard.
  2. Enter your domain name (e.g., mywebsite.com).
  3. Select a hosting plan: Shared, VPS, or Dedicated.

Tip: If you don’t own a domain yet, you can register one in the Domains section.

Troubleshooting

  • Forgot your password? Click "Forgot Password" on the login page to reset it.
  • Need help? Visit our Support Center for assistance.
