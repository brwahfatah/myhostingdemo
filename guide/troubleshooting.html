Troubleshooting

Encountering issues? Use these quick solutions to resolve common problems and get your site back online.

Website Not Loading

  • DNS Check: Ensure your domain’s DNS records are correctly configured and pointing to our servers.
  • Account Status: Verify that your hosting plan is active and not expired.
  • Server Status: Check for any ongoing maintenance or outages via your dashboard notifications.

Database Connection Error

  • Credentials: Double-check your database username, password, and host in your application settings.
  • Service Status: Make sure your database service is running and accessible.
  • Firewall Settings: Confirm that your firewall allows connections to the database port.

SSL Issues

  • Certificate Installation: Confirm your SSL certificate is properly installed via the dashboard.
  • Browser Cache: Clear your browser cache and cookies, then reload your site.
  • Renewal: Check that your SSL certificate hasn’t expired and renew if necessary.

Still Need Help?

If you’re unable to resolve the issue, contact our support team at support@yourhost.com or visit the Support Center for expert assistance.
